**BioNote**

Dr. Mariano is the Lead Faculty for Technology and Innovation at the YSEALI Academy at Fulbright University Vietnam, where he designed and leads high-impact seminars for rising leaders across Southeast Asia on topics such as AI governance, digital trust, and green technology.

He is also an Associate Editor for the IEEE Transactions on Technology and Society.

His academic foundation is extensive, holding a Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from Pennsylvania State University, an M.S. in Computer Science, and a B.S. in Statistics (magna cum laude) from the University of the Philippines Los Baños.

His leadership experience includes serving as the Director of the Institute of Computer Science at the University of the Philippines Los Baños and as the Research Coordinator for the School of Science and Technology at RMIT University Vietnam.

In the private sector, Dr. Mariano has left a significant mark as a researcher and entrepreneur. He holds three U.S. patents for his pioneering work in event detection and video analysis developed during his time at VideoMining Corporation in the United States.

 He has co-founded several technology startups, including Vinteo Inc., which specialized in retail video analysis, and Itera Robota Inc., focused on aerial imagery for agriculture  
. His consulting expertise has been sought by international organizations such as the International Rice Research Institute (IRRI), Lexmark, and Del Monte.

Beyond his technical achievements, Dr. Mariano is a dedicated educator. He has supervised over 100 research students and remains active in teaching at the Ph.D. level as an adjunct faculty member. He is also deeply committed to K-12 STEM education, frequently volunteering his time to teach coding and robotics to children.

 Recently, he successfully secured $395,000 in funding from the U.S. Department of State to lead initiatives empowering young innovators throughout the Indo-Pacific region.
